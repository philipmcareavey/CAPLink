How it works

Three sides, one platform.

Students, businesses and universities each use CAPLink differently — but every project runs through the same licensed platform, with the same safeguarding controls underneath it.

For students For businesses For universities
For students

From matched brief to being paid.

Every step below happens inside CAPLink — nothing is negotiated privately, and payment is never dependent on trust alone.

  1. See matched project briefs

    Your feed shows paid project briefs matched to the skills, interests and availability on your profile — restricted to your own university and year group's approved businesses only.

  2. Apply with a short cover note

    Apply directly to a brief that fits. The business sees your profile, your relevant skills, and how closely you match what they asked for.

  3. Agree scope and milestones

    If the business wants to go ahead, you agree the work as a set of milestones — each with its own description and payment amount — before anything starts.

  4. Do the work

    You complete each milestone and submit it for review inside the platform. Messaging with the business also runs through CAPLink.

  5. Get paid through the platform

    Once a milestone is approved, payment is released to you through CAPLink — not paid to you privately, and not dependent on the business's own invoicing process.

  6. Build a reputation from completed work

    Once work is complete, both sides leave each other a rating. Reputation from real completed work carries forward to your next application.

For businesses

From posting a brief to paying for finished work.

CAPLink is built for local, practical project work — not a general job board.

  1. Get approved by a licensed university

    Before you can reach any student, a university's careers team reviews and approves your business for a specific year group and subject area. This is the safeguarding step every business goes through — see the university safeguarding model.

  2. Post a project brief

    Describe the work, the skills it needs, the expected duration, and the rate — same as any other job posting, but scoped to a single project rather than an ongoing role.

  3. Search locally by postcode radius

    Where relevant, find students studying near your business by postcode radius, filtered by how relevant their degree is to the type of work you need.

  4. Review matched students

    See a shortlist of students matched to your brief by skills and relevance, and review anyone who's applied directly, before making a decision.

  5. Agree scope and price

    Set out the work as milestones with a description and payment amount for each, agreed with the student before work starts.

  6. Pay through the platform

    Review each completed milestone and release payment through CAPLink. You're never asked to pay a student privately or outside the platform.

For universities

From licensing to outcome reporting.

CAPLink is licensed per institution, the way you'd license any other careers or student-records software — not sold per student, and not an open marketplace your students are exposed to by default.

  1. License CAPLink for your institution

    Your institution licenses a branded portal for your own student body. See licensing options for how this is structured.

  2. Onboard your student cohort

    Students register and verify automatically against your institutional email domain — no manual ID checks needed at sign-up.

  3. Set safeguarding and access controls

    Every business that wants to reach your students requests access. Your careers team decides, per business, exactly which year groups and subject areas they can see — nothing is visible by default.

  4. Review and approve individual requests

    Approve, restrict or reject each business's request, with notes only your own team can see. A business can be approved for one year group and one subject area without being approved for any other.

  5. Students apply and work, safely inside the same controls

    Everything downstream — matching, applying, messaging, payment — happens only within the access your team has already set.

  6. Receive outcome reporting

    Get reporting on placements, sectors and outcomes specific to your own students — usable as real evidence for your institution's own accountability.

How matching works

Matched by relevance, not by who applies first.

CAPLink matches a student's declared skills, interests and availability against a business's project brief, and factors in a student's reputation from previously completed work. The detail of how that scoring works stays internal — what matters publicly is that it's relevance-based, not a first-come, first-served queue, and that a business always sees why a candidate was suggested to them.
