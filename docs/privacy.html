1. Who we are (the controller)

("CAPLink", "we", "us") is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. We are a company registered in England and Wales, company number , registered office . Our data protection contact is contact.html#data-protection.

2. Personal data we collect

What we collect depends on which role you use CAPLink in.

Students

  • Account details: name, university email address, hashed password, university and year group/band.
  • Profile details: degree title, skills, hourly rate expectation, weekly availability, and — where provided — a postcode used only to support local business search.
  • Activity data: applications submitted, contracts and milestones you're party to, messages you send and receive, ratings you give and receive, and records of recommendations shown to you and how you acted on them.
  • Payment details: handled directly by our payment processor (Stripe) — see who we share data with — we hold a reference to your Stripe Connect account, not your bank details directly.
  • Consent record: an explicit, timestamped record of your consent to your university receiving engagement and outcome data about you, captured at registration.
  • Device data, if you use a mobile app: a device token used to deliver notifications, and basic app version information.

Business contacts

  • Account and company details: your name, work email, hashed password, and your company's name, registration number, industry, size, website and description, where provided.
  • Location data, if provided: a postcode used to support local search by students and universities, geocoded to an approximate location.
  • Activity data: projects posted, applications reviewed, contracts and milestones, messages, and ratings given and received.
  • Payment details: handled directly by Stripe — we hold a reference to your saved payment method, not your card details directly.

University staff

  • Account details: name, work email, hashed password, and role (careers/admissions staff or platform administrator).
  • Configuration data you enter on your institution's behalf: business partnership decisions, permitted year groups and subject categories, campus location, and (if configured) your institution's single sign-on settings.
  • Where single sign-on is used, the attributes your institution's identity provider sends us at login (typically email, display name, and an affiliation/role claim) — see who we share data with.

We do not knowingly collect more personal data than a specific feature needs — see our internal minimisation review referenced in the site's technical documentation. a plain-English summary of that review for a non-technical audience can be added here if useful.

4. Who we share data with

We share personal data only with the following categories of recipient, each acting as our processor unless stated otherwise:

  • Payment processing: Stripe, Inc. (Stripe Connect) — processes all payments and payouts. We do not store full card or bank account details ourselves.
  • Payroll / employer-of-record (PAYE route): — for visa-restricted students routed through PAYE payroll rather than paid as self-employed. No provider is under contract yet; this section will be updated once one is chosen.
  • Hosting and infrastructure: our application and database are hosted with Render (render.com). Render encrypts data at rest.
  • Error tracking: Sentry — used to capture application errors so we can fix them; configured not to send personal data by default.
  • Bot/abuse protection: hCaptcha — used on registration forms to reduce automated abuse.
  • Password breach checking: the Have I Been Pwned Pwned Passwords service — checked using only a partial, irreversible hash of a password you choose (k-anonymity), never the password itself or any other personal data.
  • Email delivery: — no email service provider is live yet; verification emails are not currently sent by a third party.
  • Push notifications (mobile): — not yet active.
  • Your own university: where you're a student, your university's careers/admissions staff can see the engagement and outcome data covered by your consent (see lawful basis above), and — separately — safeguarding access to which businesses can reach which year groups is controlled entirely by your university's own staff.
  • Regulators and law enforcement: where we're legally required to.

We do not sell personal data, and do not share it with any recipient not listed above.

6. How long we keep data

An account that never completes email verificationDeleted after 30 days
An account inactive for an extended periodAnonymised after 24 months of inactivity (see below)
Recommendation/matching logs (what was shown, and the outcome)Purged after 12 months
Contracts, milestones, ratings and messagesRetained for as long as the account (or the other party's account) is active — see below for what happens on account closure
Audit logs of safeguarding and moderation decisions

When you close your account, or ask us to delete it, we anonymise your account rather than deleting the underlying records outright: your name, email, password and any device tokens are cleared, but a contract, rating or message you were party to is left in place — pointing at an anonymised account — because it also legitimately belongs to the record of the other party involved (their own contract history, their own received rating). This reflects the right to erasure's actual scope: erasing what identifies you, not data another party has a continuing legitimate interest in.

7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is used (this policy);
  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased, in the circumstances described above;
  • restrict or object to certain processing, including processing based on legitimate interests;
  • data portability, for data you provided to us and that we process by consent or under a contract;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent (this won't affect processing already carried out).

You can request an export of your own data, or ask us to close and anonymise your account, directly from your account settings, or by contacting our data protection contact.

8. Complaints to the ICO

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your personal data, we'd like the chance to put it right first — contact us at contact.html#data-protection. You also have the right to complain directly to the UK's supervisory authority, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

9. Automated decision-making

Flagged for the site owner's own review before publication. The wording below states a legal conclusion (that CAPLink's matching engine does not fall within UK GDPR Article 22) — this is exactly the kind of statement that needs sign-off from someone qualified to make it, not just a description of how the software behaves.

CAPLink's matching engine ranks and suggests candidates and projects to help students and businesses decide who to work with — it does not decide anything on its own. A business always makes its own decision about who to hire, and a student always makes their own decision about which project to apply to; a university's safeguarding decisions about which businesses can reach which students are made by a human member of staff, not by the software. We don't consider this automated decision-making producing legal or similarly significant effects within the meaning of UK GDPR Article 22, since a human decision sits on both sides of every outcome that actually affects you. If that understanding is ever wrong for a specific feature, this section needs to be corrected and, where required, the rights in Article 22 (including the right to a human review) made available for that specific decision.

10. Under-18 students

Flagged for review. The platform does not currently collect or verify date of birth for any user, including students. This section is an honest description of that gap, not a claim that under-18 safeguards are fully solved.

CAPLink registers students using their university-issued email address, and relies on the fact that account access is restricted to people your university has enrolled. We do not separately collect or verify a student's date of birth, and the platform does not currently have an under-18-specific consent or safeguarding flow of its own. Where a university's own student population includes under-18 students (for example, some foundation-year or access-course students), responsibility for any additional safeguarding requirements for those students rests with the university's own admissions and safeguarding processes, in addition to the year-group/business-access controls CAPLink provides. whether a dedicated under-18 consent flow is required should be confirmed with a solicitor and, if so, scoped as real product work rather than described here as already solved.

11. How we protect your data

Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text, and checked against a live breach database at registration. Data in our production database is encrypted at rest. Access to a university's own student data is restricted to that university's own staff and to CAPLink's own platform administrators. We use rate limiting, bot protection, and an audit log of sensitive administrative actions. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security of information transmitted over the internet.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll update the "last updated" date above when we do, and — for material changes — aim to let registered users know directly rather than relying on this page alone.
