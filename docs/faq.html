Students

Is the work paid?

Yes. Every project on CAPLink runs as a paid, milestone-based agreement between you and the business — there's no unpaid "exposure" listing category, and a project isn't visible to you at all unless it has a payment amount attached to it.

How and when do I get paid?

Work is broken into milestones, each with its own agreed payment amount. When you submit a milestone and the business approves it, payment is released to you through CAPLink's payment processor rather than paid to you directly by the business.

Typical time from milestone approval to funds reaching your account, and which payment methods are supported, to be confirmed here.

Does this affect my student finance or my visa?

If you're on a student visa with a weekly working-hours limit, CAPLink routes your pay through a PAYE/umbrella payroll arrangement rather than treating you as self-employed, so your working pattern stays within your visa conditions. Specific guidance on student finance and visa working-hours rules should be confirmed with your university and, for visa questions, UKCISA or your institution's international student support team before relying on this page alone.

Do I need to be at a partner university?

Yes. CAPLink only registers students against a university that has licensed the platform, using your institutional email address. If your university isn't a partner yet, let us know and we can get in touch with your careers service.

What if a project goes wrong?

Message the business directly through CAPLink first — most issues are scope or communication problems that get resolved that way. If a milestone genuinely isn't delivered, the business can decline to approve it rather than being forced to pay for undelivered work; if you believe that's unfair, or the business is unresponsive, contact us and we'll look into it.

Businesses

What does it cost?

Business-side pricing (a platform fee on completed work, a subscription, or both) is still being finalised and will be published here before general availability rather than left to guesswork.

How do I know a student can do the work?

Every student profile shows their course, relevant skills, and — once they've completed prior work on the platform — a reputation built from real completed projects, not self-reported claims alone. You can also review a shortlist of matched candidates before deciding who to work with.

Who is the employer?

For most projects, the student is engaged on a self-employed basis directly with your business for that project — CAPLink is the platform the agreement and payment run through, not the employer. Where a student is visa-restricted, their pay is instead routed through a PAYE/umbrella payroll arrangement rather than treated as self-employment — see the Payments, tax and legal section below.

What if I'm not happy with the work?

You only release payment for a milestone once you've reviewed and approved it, so you're never forced to pay for undelivered work. If a submitted milestone doesn't meet what was agreed, you can decline it and work with the student to resolve it through messaging before resubmission.

Universities

How does licensing work?

CAPLink is licensed annually, per institution, rather than sold per student — see licensing options for the tiers currently offered. Get in touch to discuss a pilot for your institution.

What data do you hold on our students?

Profile information the student provides at registration (course, year group, skills, contact details), plus records of applications, agreed work and payments they're party to. Full detail, including lawful basis and retention periods, is in our privacy policy.

How does this support OfS condition B3 reporting?

CAPLink's employability reporting gives your careers team placement, sector and outcome data specific to your own students, which your institution can use as part of its own graduate-outcomes evidence base. A specific mapping of CAPLink's reporting fields to condition B3's evidence requirements should be confirmed directly with your institution's own quality/compliance team before being relied on for a submission.

What safeguarding controls exist?

No business can see or contact any of your students until your careers team has approved that business for a specific year group and subject area. See the safeguarding model on our university page and the band-control walkthrough on how it works.

Payments, tax and legal

Who processes payments?

Payments are processed through Stripe Connect. Businesses pay via a saved card; students receive payment into a Stripe-connected account (or, for visa-restricted students, via a PAYE/umbrella payroll route — see below).

How is tax handled?

Most students are paid on a self-employed basis and are responsible for declaring and paying their own tax on that income, the same as any other self-employed work. Visa-restricted students are instead paid through a PAYE/umbrella payroll arrangement, which handles tax and National Insurance through payroll rather than self-assessment. The named umbrella/payroll provider has not yet been chosen — this section will be updated once one is under contract. Nothing here is tax advice; students and businesses should confirm their own position with HMRC or an accountant.

What about international students?

International students on a visa with working-hours restrictions are automatically routed to the PAYE/umbrella payroll rail rather than paid as self-employed, so their working pattern stays within their visa's conditions. Students should still confirm their own visa working-hours limits with their university's international student support team or UKCISA — this page doesn't replace that advice.
