Why careers teams choose CAPLink

Every other platform asks you to trust the market.
CAPLink lets you set the terms.

Open marketplaces expose every student to every employer, regardless of year group, relevance, or how well that business has been vetted. CAPLink flips that: no business reaches your students until your team says so.

Without controls

A first-year is discoverable by any employer, including ones your institution has never reviewed.

Without controls

Businesses can post roles wildly outside a student's course relevance — unrelated sales gigs to an engineering cohort.

Without controls

Your employability data lives entirely on someone else's platform, with no reporting back to your team.

With CAPLink

Each business is approved per year-band by your team. First-years can be restricted to your most trusted, education-focused partners — or excluded entirely.

With CAPLink

Businesses are approved per project category too — a marketing agency can be cleared for marketing work only, not engineering placements.

With CAPLink

Your institution gets its own employability dashboard: placements, sectors, and outcomes, reportable to your leadership.
