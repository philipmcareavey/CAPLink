Short of a full hire, but you've got real project work?

By · · 5 min read · ← Back to blog

A lot of useful work inside a small or mid-sized business never gets done, not because it isn't worth doing, but because it doesn't clear the bar for a proper hire. A landing page that needs rebuilding. A spreadsheet of customer data that needs cleaning and structuring properly. A piece of market research before a decision gets made. Each one is genuinely useful, each one is genuinely bounded, and none of them justifies advertising a role, running interviews, and taking on a permanent member of staff.

That gap — real, useful, bounded work with no efficient way to resource it — is exactly what CAPLink is built to close, using students studying nearby who have both the relevant skills and the time.

Why a student, specifically

Students bring current, relevant skills — the data analysis, design, or research methods they're actively studying, not a decade-old version of them — and they're looking for exactly this shape of work: bounded, paid, and relevant to their course, not a long commitment they can't take on alongside their studies.

What the process actually looks like

  • Get approved by a partner university. Before you can reach any student, a university's careers team reviews your business and approves you for a specific year group and subject area — this is the safeguarding step every business goes through, not an optional extra.
  • Post the brief. Describe the work, the skills it needs, and the rate — scoped to a single project, not an ongoing role.
  • Search locally, if that matters for the work. Find students studying near your business by postcode radius, filtered by how relevant their course is to what you need.
  • Review a shortlist matched to the brief, and anyone who's applied directly.
  • Agree the work as milestones — a description and a price for each — before anything starts.
  • Pay through the platform once each milestone is delivered and reviewed. You're never asked to pay a student privately.

What it isn't

This isn't a staffing agency arrangement, and CAPLink doesn't manage the work day to day on your behalf — you brief the work and review what comes back, same as you would with any contractor. It's also not an open marketplace: no business reaches any student without a university having approved that access first, which is exactly the control that makes this different from posting a generic freelance listing.

if you want to know whether a specific university near you is already a partner, or what getting approved involves in practice, get in touch and we'll walk you through it directly.
