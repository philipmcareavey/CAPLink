Terms of use

Part A — Site terms

This part covers simply browsing this website — before you register or hold any account. By using this site you agree to use it lawfully, not to attempt to disrupt or gain unauthorised access to it, and not to copy or republish its content without permission other than for ordinary personal, non-commercial reference. Content on this site is provided for general information and is not itself an offer or contract — a specific agreement (e.g. a university licence, or a project agreed between a student and a business) is what actually governs that relationship.

Part B — Platform terms

This part covers using the CAPLink platform itself, once you have a student, business or university account.

Eligibility

  • Students must be enrolled at a university that has licensed CAPLink, and register using their institutional email address.
  • Businesses must be approved by at least one licensed university's careers team before they can post any project or contact any student.
  • University staff accounts are provisioned by CAPLink or by the institution's own single sign-on, on the institution's authority.

Acceptable use

You agree not to:

  • use CAPLink to arrange or solicit work outside a properly agreed contract on the platform;
  • attempt to move an agreed engagement off-platform to avoid CAPLink's payment, safeguarding or moderation controls (see the off-platform-contact flagging in our messaging feature);
  • post project briefs, messages or profile content that is unlawful, harassing, discriminatory, or materially misleading;
  • attempt to circumvent a university's access controls over which students a business can reach;
  • misuse the platform's matching, messaging or payment systems, including attempting to interfere with their operation.

Account responsibilities

You're responsible for keeping your account credentials secure and for activity that happens under your account. Tell us immediately if you believe your account has been compromised. University staff accounts are also subject to whatever access policy their own institution sets internally.

The relationship between parties

CAPLink is the platform the agreement, work and payment run through — it is not the employer, and it is not the client. For self-employed-rail work, the contractual relationship for the work itself is between the student and the business directly; CAPLink facilitates the introduction, the agreed milestone terms, and the payment flow. Where a student is routed to the PAYE/umbrella payroll rail (see our FAQ), a separate payroll relationship applies via that provider rather than direct self-employment. A university's role is to license the platform and control safeguarding access for its own students; a university is not a party to the contract between a specific student and business.

Payments and fees

Payment for agreed work is processed through CAPLink's payment processor (Stripe). A milestone amount is authorised when a contract is created and released to the student once the business approves the completed milestone.

CAPLink's own platform fee (charged to the business, the student, or both, and at what rate) is not yet finalised and will be stated here, not left implied, before this page is published live. University licence fees are separate and covered by each institution's own licence agreement, not by these terms.

Intellectual property in work produced

The default position on who owns intellectual property in work a student produces for a business (for example, whether it assigns to the business on payment, as is common for commissioned freelance work) has not yet been fixed and must not be assumed here. Once confirmed, this section should state the default plainly and note that a contract's own terms can vary it where agreed by both parties.

Liability

CAPLink provides the platform, matching, and payment infrastructure, but does not itself perform, supervise or guarantee the quality of any work agreed between a student and a business. Specific liability caps and exclusions (for example, excluding liability for indirect or consequential loss, and any cap by reference to fees paid) need drafting and reviewing by a solicitor rather than estimated here — nothing in this section should be treated as the final wording.

Suspension and termination

We may suspend or terminate an account that breaches acceptable use above, that a university withdraws safeguarding approval for, or that we reasonably believe poses a risk to another user or to the platform. Where practical, we'll tell you why. You can close your own account at any time — see our privacy policy for what happens to your data when you do.

Changes to these terms

Notice period before a change to these terms takes effect, and how continuing to use CAPLink after that point will be treated, should be set here rather than left unstated.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising from them.
