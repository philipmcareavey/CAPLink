About CAPLink

A licensed platform for paid student work — not a marketplace, not an agency.

CAPLink exists to solve one specific, recurring problem for three different people at once: a student who needs real paid experience, a local business that needs affordable project capacity, and a university that needs to show its careers service actually produces outcomes.

The three-sided problem

Three groups, one shared gap.

Each of these is a real, separate problem — CAPLink is built around solving all three at once, not one at the expense of the others.

Students

Employers increasingly expect demonstrable work experience before graduation, but paid, relevant, short-term project work is hard to find — most of what's advertised is either unpaid, generic, or requires experience the student doesn't have yet.

Local businesses

Small and mid-sized businesses often have real project work — a data cleanup, a landing page, a piece of research — that doesn't justify a full hire, but they have no efficient, trustworthy way to find and brief a student for it.

Universities

Careers services are increasingly expected to evidence graduate outcomes, not just run generic listings boards — but most existing platforms give a university no visibility, no control over which employers reach which students, and no reporting back.

How CAPLink addresses it

Students apply to real, paid project briefs matched to their skills and interests, build a reputation from completed work, and get paid through the platform on milestone approval — not on trust.

How CAPLink addresses it

Businesses post a brief, search for students by postcode radius, review a shortlist matched to the work, and agree scope and price before anything starts — all inside one licensed platform, not a cold-outreach process.

How CAPLink addresses it

A university licenses CAPLink for its own student body, controls exactly which businesses can reach which year groups, and receives outcome reporting it can use for its own accountability — see how it works for the full flow.

How the model works

One licensed platform, three roles.

A university licenses CAPLink the way it would license any other institutional software. Its careers team then controls which businesses can reach which students, at what year group, in what subject area — before a business ever sees a single student. Students apply to matched project briefs; businesses post briefs, review matched candidates, and agree terms; payment for completed work runs through the platform rather than being negotiated privately.

What we're not

Not a recruitment agency. Not a temp desk.

01

Not a recruitment agency

CAPLink doesn't source, shortlist or place candidates on a business's behalf, and doesn't charge a placement fee for doing so. Students apply directly to briefs; businesses review and decide directly.

02

Not a temp/staffing desk

CAPLink is not the employer of record for self-employed-rail work, and doesn't manage shifts, rotas or day-to-day supervision. It's the platform the agreement, the work, and the payment run through.

03

Not an open marketplace

No business reaches any student until a university has approved that business for that student's year group and subject area. See the safeguarding model on our university page.
