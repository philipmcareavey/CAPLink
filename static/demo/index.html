Why careers teams choose Bridge

Every open marketplace asks you to trust it.
Bridge lets a university set the terms.

Open marketplaces expose every student to every employer, regardless of year group, relevance, or how well that business has actually been vetted. Bridge flips that around: a business has no visibility of any student until the student's university explicitly says so.

Without a safeguarding gate

A first-year is discoverable by any registered business, including ones the university has never reviewed.

Without a safeguarding gate

A business can post roles wildly outside a student's course relevance — an unrelated sales gig pitched to an engineering cohort.

Without a safeguarding gate

Students can be lured off-platform into informal, unprotected payment arrangements before any contract exists.

With Bridge

Each business is approved per year-band by the university's careers team — first-years can be restricted to trusted, education-focused partners, or excluded entirely.

With Bridge

Businesses are approved per project category too — a marketing agency can be cleared for marketing work only, never engineering placements.

With Bridge

Messages are automatically scanned pre-contract for phone numbers, external emails, and "pay you directly" language, and flagged for review.

The lifecycle

From approval to payout, every step is logged.

The same rules apply whether a student is browsing on the web or on their phone — nothing about access or safeguarding changes by device.

01

Safeguarding gate

No business reaches a student until an approved agreement exists, scoped to specific year-bands and project categories.

02

Explainable matching

Every recommendation — to a student or a business — ships with plain-English reasons, and is logged to train future versions of the matching engine.

03

Milestone escrow payments

Contracts are broken into milestones. A student is paid when a business approves the work, never just on a promise.

04

Mutual blind ratings

Both sides rate each other after a contract — but neither sees the other's rating until both have submitted theirs.

05

Off-platform-contact detection

Pre-contract messages are scanned for attempts to move a conversation off-platform, protecting students and the integrity of the agreement.

06

Mobile-ready by design

Paginated feeds, long-lived refresh tokens, and a single combined home payload — built for a phone on a patchy connection, not just a desktop.

A note on what this actually is

This is a working reference demo, not a live product.

Everything below the login screen calls a real local API and a real database — the safeguarding rules, matching, contracts, and payments logic all genuinely run. A few integration points are deliberately left as clearly-marked placeholders.

Fully working

  • Registration, login, JWT refresh
  • Multi-tenant university licensing
  • The safeguarding access-control gate
  • Rules-based matching + recommendation logging
  • Applications, contracts, milestones
  • Mutual blind ratings
  • Messaging with off-platform-contact flagging

Placeholders

  • Stripe payment capture (a fake payment id is stored instead)
  • Firebase push notifications (logged, not actually sent)
  • University email verification (domain match only, no real SSO)
  • Schema migrations (tables are created fresh each run)
